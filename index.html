Who we are

Featured project · Team 15

Eli Lilly: Manufacturing Capacity Planning

Our current project explores how a decision-support application could help manufacturing planners compare forecasted demand, inventory, and production capacity for medications such as Mounjaro and Zepbound.

Using Dify.ai and clearly labeled simulated data, our proposed workflow identifies projected supply gaps, classifies shortage risk, and explains potential planning actions. The prototype is designed to support human decisions, not replace regulated manufacturing or quality processes.
